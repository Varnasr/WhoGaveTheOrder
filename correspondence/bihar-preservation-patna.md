**Subject: Written request for preservation of records relating to police action at JP Golambar, Patna on 22 July 2026 and at Dakbungalow Square, Patna on 25 July 2026**

Sir/Madam,

1. I write to request, in writing and on record, that the documents and recordings identified in paragraph 5 below be preserved and not destroyed, overwritten, deleted or otherwise disposed of in the ordinary course of any retention schedule, pending the matters set out below.

2. This representation concerns two incidents:

   (a) police action at JP Golambar, Patna, on **22 July 2026**; and

   (b) police action at Dakbungalow Square, Patna, on **25 July 2026**, during a Bihar Bandh, in the course of which 17 persons are reported to have been detained.

   Both fall within the student protests in Bihar of 21 to 27 July 2026.

3. I do not, in this letter, make any allegation against any officer, and nothing here should be read as one. I am not asking your office to accept any account of what occurred. I am asking only that the record of what occurred be kept.

4. The reason this request is made now is that the police action against the protesters of this period is the subject of an inquiry constituted by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India. By an order reported dated 18 August 2026 in the proceedings concerning the police action against the protesters, the Court constituted a five-member High Powered Enquiry Committee chaired by Justice R. Subhash Reddy, formerly of that Court. The State of Bihar is a respondent in those proceedings and has filed an affidavit in them. Records of the kind listed below are liable to be material to that inquiry. Their loss to a routine retention cycle, once the inquiry exists, is not recoverable.

5. **Records requested to be preserved**, for the period from 00:00 hours on 21 July 2026 to 23:59 hours on 27 July 2026, in respect of the two locations named in paragraph 2 and the police stations in whose jurisdiction they fall:

   a. Control room audio recordings and control room logs.
   b. Wireless and radio communication logs, and any recordings of them.
   c. CCTV footage from cameras owned, operated or controlled by the police, and any CCTV footage already obtained by the police from any other person or body.
   d. Body-worn camera recordings, if body-worn cameras were in use.
   e. Videography of the police action, including any recording made by or for the police under any standing order or standard operating procedure.
   f. Deployment orders, duty rosters and force requisitions, including any requisition of armed force and any written order authorising the use of force.
   g. General diary entries, station diary entries and case diaries relating to the two incidents.
   h. Any order under section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, in force at either location on the dates in question.
   i. Records of the 17 persons reported detained on 25 July 2026, including arrest memos, medical examination records and the times of detention and release.

6. I am separately making an application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 for the record retention schedule applicable to the categories above. This representation is made ahead of that application precisely because the schedule is not yet known to me, and a record may be destroyed on schedule while the question of the schedule is being answered.

7. I am aware that this representation is not a statutory instrument and that it does not by itself create a duty. I make it so that the date on which your office was informed is fixed. If any of the records listed above has already been destroyed, I request that I be informed of that fact and of the date and authority of the destruction, which is itself information I am entitled to seek.

8. I would be grateful for an acknowledgement of this letter, and for the name and designation of the officer to whom any follow-up should be addressed.
