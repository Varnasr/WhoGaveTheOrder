**Subject: Written request for preservation of records relating to protest activity at Munger on 23 July 2026**

Sir/Madam,

1. I write to request, in writing and on record, that the documents and recordings identified in paragraph 5 below be preserved and not destroyed, overwritten, deleted or otherwise disposed of in the ordinary course of any retention schedule, pending the matters set out below.

2. This representation concerns **protest activity at Munger on 23 July 2026**, during the student protests in Bihar of 21 to 27 July 2026. I should be clear about what I am and am not saying. I have seen a report of protest activity at Munger on that date. I have **not** established that police force was used at Munger, and I do not assert that it was. It is precisely because I cannot say either way that the records matter: they are what would settle it, and they are what a retention cycle will remove.

3. I make no allegation against any officer, and nothing here should be read as one.

4. The reason this request is made now is that the police action against the protesters of this period is the subject of an inquiry constituted by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India. By an order reported dated 18 August 2026 in the proceedings concerning the police action against the protesters, the Court constituted a five-member High Powered Enquiry Committee chaired by Justice R. Subhash Reddy, formerly of that Court. The State of Bihar is a respondent in those proceedings and has filed an affidavit in them. Records of the kind listed below are liable to be material to that inquiry, including where they establish that nothing occurred.

5. **Records requested to be preserved**, for the period from 00:00 hours on 22 July 2026 to 23:59 hours on 24 July 2026, in respect of Munger town and the police stations in whose jurisdiction any protest activity on that date fell:

   a. Control room audio recordings and control room logs.
   b. Wireless and radio communication logs, and any recordings of them.
   c. CCTV footage from cameras owned, operated or controlled by the police, and any CCTV footage already obtained by the police from any other person or body.
   d. Body-worn camera recordings, if body-worn cameras were in use.
   e. Any videography of the protest or of any police response to it.
   f. Deployment orders, duty rosters and force requisitions for the date, including any requisition of armed force and any written order authorising the use of force.
   g. General diary entries and station diary entries for the date.
   h. Any order under section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, in force at Munger on the date in question.
   i. Arrest memos, medical examination records, and the times of detention and release, in respect of any person detained in connection with the protest.

6. I am separately making an application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 for the record retention schedule applicable to the categories above. This representation is made ahead of that application precisely because the schedule is not yet known to me, and a record may be destroyed on schedule while the question of the schedule is being answered.

7. I am aware that this representation is not a statutory instrument and that it does not by itself create a duty. I make it so that the date on which your office was informed is fixed. If any of the records listed above has already been destroyed, I request that I be informed of that fact and of the date and authority of the destruction, which is itself information I am entitled to seek.

8. I would be grateful for an acknowledgement of this letter, and for the name and designation of the officer to whom any follow-up should be addressed.
