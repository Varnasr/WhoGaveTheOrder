**Subject: Written request for preservation of records relating to the police action and discharge of firearms at Siwan on 25 July 2026**

Sir/Madam,

1. I write to request, in writing and on record, that the documents and recordings identified in paragraph 6 below be preserved and not destroyed, overwritten, deleted or otherwise disposed of in the ordinary course of any retention schedule, pending the matters set out below.

2. This representation concerns the police action during the student protest at **Siwan on 25 July 2026**, in the course of which police discharged firearms.

3. That firearms were discharged is not in dispute between me and the State. The State of Bihar's own account, given on affidavit in the proceedings before the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India, is that four rounds were fired from an AK-47 near JP Chowk and two rounds from a 9mm pistol near Hathi Chowk, and that three protesters sustained firearm injuries. I proceed on that account and make no allegation against any officer.

4. What is not settled is how many rounds were fired, from how many weapons, and at how many locations. A published open-source analysis based on geolocated bystander video reports a materially different count. I take no position on which account is correct. The point of this letter is that the records held by your office are what would resolve it, and that they are the records a retention cycle removes first.

5. The police action against the protesters of this period is the subject of an inquiry constituted by the Hon'ble Supreme Court. By an order reported dated 18 August 2026, the Court constituted a five-member High Powered Enquiry Committee chaired by Justice R. Subhash Reddy, formerly of that Court. The State of Bihar is a respondent in those proceedings and has filed the affidavit referred to in paragraph 3. Records of the kind listed below are liable to be material to that inquiry.

6. **Records requested to be preserved**, for the period from 00:00 hours on 24 July 2026 to 23:59 hours on 26 July 2026, in respect of Siwan town, including JP Chowk, Hathi Chowk, the district commandant's office and the Kagzi Mohalla to Naya Bazaar road, and the police stations in whose jurisdiction they fall:

   a. Control room audio recordings and control room logs.
   b. Wireless and radio communication logs, and any recordings of them.
   c. CCTV footage from cameras owned, operated or controlled by the police, and any CCTV footage already obtained by the police from any other person or body.
   d. Body-worn camera recordings, if body-worn cameras were in use.
   e. Videography of the police action, including any recording made by or for the police under any standing order or standard operating procedure.
   f. Deployment orders, duty rosters and force requisitions, including any requisition of armed force and any written order authorising the use of force or the use of firearms.
   g. **Arms and ammunition records**: the issue and return registers for weapons drawn on the date, the rounds issued and returned, the magazine and ammunition accounting, and the identity by designation of the officers to whom the weapons named in the State's affidavit were issued.
   h. Any report of the discharge of firearms made under any standing order, police manual provision or departmental instruction, and any magisterial or departmental inquiry record arising from it.
   i. Medico-legal case records and injury reports in respect of the persons who sustained firearm injuries, and the records of the hospital to which they were taken, so far as your office holds or has obtained them.
   j. General diary entries, station diary entries and case diaries relating to the incident, and the FIRs registered in connection with the events at Siwan on that date.
   k. Any order under section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, in force at Siwan on the date in question.

7. I am separately making an application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 for the record retention schedule applicable to the categories above. This representation is made ahead of that application precisely because the schedule is not yet known to me, and a record may be destroyed on schedule while the question of the schedule is being answered.

8. I am aware that this representation is not a statutory instrument and that it does not by itself create a duty. I make it so that the date on which your office was informed is fixed. If any of the records listed above has already been destroyed, I request that I be informed of that fact and of the date and authority of the destruction, which is itself information I am entitled to seek.

9. I would be grateful for an acknowledgement of this letter, and for the name and designation of the officer to whom any follow-up should be addressed.
