The signature feature

Follow the chain of command

Authority runs from the Union, through the state, through senior command, to the officers on the ground. The chain shows who is empowered to decide what, and stops there.

Holding an office is not evidence of having issued an order. These are two separate claims and the interface keeps them separate. Until a document places an office behind a specific action, the node reads authorisation not established.
