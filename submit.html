    Before you send anything

    What happens to what you send

    Nothing submitted by the public goes live automatically.

    Your safety comes before our archive. Do not break a law, a service rule or a confidentiality obligation to send us something. Do not photograph a document somewhere you can be identified doing it. If a document can be obtained through an RTI application instead, tell us and we will file it ourselves.

    Contact details are stored privately. They are never published, never shown to a third party, and never attached to a published record. If you ask us not to contact you, we will not, even where it costs us the verification.
